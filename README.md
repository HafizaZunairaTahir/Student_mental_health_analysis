Student Mental Health: A Multivariate Data Investigation

As a 15-year-old Hafiza, Martial Artist, and Aspiring AI Founder, I believe data is the key to solving human crises. This project is an end-to-end investigation into university student wellness, specifically uncovering the "Triple Threat"—the dangerous intersection where Depression, Anxiety, and Panic Attacks overlap.

Key Findings:

The 9.9% Crisis: My analysis revealed that nearly 1 in 10 students suffer from all three conditions simultaneously.

The Topper’s Tax: Data shows a significant correlation between high academic performance (CGPA 3.5+) and increased anxiety levels.

Actionable Insights: I proposed four data-driven wellness strategies to bridge the gap in professional help-seeking behavior.

Technical Workflow:
Using a Topper mindset of precision and grit, I utilized Python (Pandas, Seaborn, Plotly) to transform raw survey data into interactive visualizations. This project follows professional industry standards, from data cleaning to multivariate correlation analysis.

Vision:
This repository serves as a foundation for my future AI startup. I am committed to building intelligent systems that prioritize human well-being through technical excellence.
